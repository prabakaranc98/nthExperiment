## Key papers

Canonical methods plus the 2024-2026 work that defines the current frontier.

### Foundations (timeless)

| Resource | What it is + why | Level |
|----------|------------------|-------|
| [Toward Causal Representation Learning (Schölkopf et al.)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.11107) | The CRL manifesto — independent mechanisms, disentanglement, the link to OOD generalization and transfer. | 🔴 |
| [Invariant Risk Minimization (Arjovsky et al.)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.02893) | Learn representations invariant across environments — causality meets OOD generalization. The IRM paper and the starting point for the invariance literature. | 🔴 |
| [Double/Debiased Machine Learning (Chernozhukov et al.)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.00060) | Neyman-orthogonal estimators that plug ML into effect estimation with valid inference. The rigorous applied-causal workhorse. | 🔴 |

### Identifiability & CRL (2023-2026)

| Resource | What it is + why | Level |
|----------|------------------|-------|
| [Nonparametric Identifiability of Causal Representations from Unknown Interventions (von Kügelgen et al., NeurIPS 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.00542) | Proves latent causal variables are identifiable from data generated by unknown single-node interventions — the theoretical backbone of modern interventional CRL. | 🔴 |
| [Multi-View Causal Representation Learning (Yao, von Kügelgen et al.)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.04056) | Identifies shared latent causal factors across multiple measurement views — connects CRL to multimodal/contrastive learning. | 🔴 |
| [Causal Component Analysis (Liang, von Kügelgen et al.)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.17225) | An intermediate problem between ICA and full CRL — recover causal mechanisms when the causal graph is known but the mixing is not. | 🔴 |
| Causal de Finetti / exchangeability-based identifiability (Guo, Tóth, Schölkopf, Huszár) | Shows i.i.d.-violating exchangeable data lets you identify causal structure without interventions — a clean bridge between Bayesian exchangeability and causal discovery. Search the authors' arXiv listings. | 🔴 |

### Causal discovery & LLMs (2024-2026)

| Resource | What it is + why | Level |
|----------|------------------|-------|
| [DiffAN / diffusion-based causal discovery — Sanchez et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.06169) | Use the learned score (diffusion) to read off the topological order of an additive-noise SEM — score-based discovery that scales to many variables. | 🔴 |
| Causal inference with / for LLMs (Zhang, Jin, Schölkopf et al.) | Survey-and-critique line on whether LLMs do causal reasoning vs. recite causal facts, and how to wire formal causal tools to them. Named without a fixed URL — check the authors' recent arXiv. | 🟡 |
| Benchmarks: CLadder, Corr2Cause, CausalBench | The standard probes for causal reasoning in LLMs (formal NLP queries, correlation-to-causation, gene-network discovery). Look up each by name; versions update frequently. | 🟡 |

## The Bayesian / representation connection

Causal estimation leans on Bayesian regression, GPs, and information theory — these make that machinery intuitive.

| Resource | What it is + why | Level |
|----------|------------------|-------|
| [Distill: A Visual Exploration of Gaussian Processes](https://distill.pub/2019/visual-exploration-gaussian-processes/) | GPs as distributions over functions — the Bayesian regression framework under many causal estimators. | 🟡 |
| [Distill: Exploring Bayesian Optimization](https://distill.pub/2020/bayesian-optimization/) | Sequential decision-making under uncertainty; the bridge from probabilistic modeling to causal decision-making. | 🟡 |
| [Colah: Visual Information Theory](https://colah.github.io/posts/2015-09-Visual-Information/) | The information-theoretic lens linking Bayesian inference, causality, and representation learning. | 🟢 |
