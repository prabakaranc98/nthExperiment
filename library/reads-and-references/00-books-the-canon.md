# The Canon — Seminal Books for DS / ML / AI

The books worth owning and returning to — the ones that build durable understanding, not just recipes. 📖 = legally free online (read these first; no excuse not to).

**FAIRE context:** the deep-reading spine under the [Program Handbook](../../faire-program.md). Pair with the per-topic web [reads-and-references](README.md) and the [bricks](../bricks/README.md) for cold lookup.

## Start here (the desert-island shortlist)

| Book | Why | |
|------|-----|---|
| Blum, Hopcroft & Kannan — *Foundations of Data Science* | The math of modern DS: high-dim geometry, random projection, SVD, random walks, streaming, clustering. The text for [Algorithms for AI/ML/DS](../../seminars/algorithms-for-ai-ml-ds.md). | [📖](https://home.ttic.edu/~avrim/book.pdf) |
| Hastie, Tibshirani & Friedman — *The Elements of Statistical Learning* | The statistical-ML bible; the reference everyone cites. | [📖](https://hastie.su.domains/ElemStatLearn/) |
| Murphy — *Probabilistic Machine Learning* (vol. 1 & 2) | The modern, comprehensive, probabilistic treatment — the best single ML reference of the 2020s. | [📖](https://probml.github.io/pml-book/) |
| Goodfellow, Bengio & Courville — *Deep Learning* | The foundational DL text; still the best for the fundamentals. | [📖](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/) |
| MacKay — *Information Theory, Inference & Learning Algorithms* | Information theory and inference, taught with unmatched clarity — this repo's spine. | [📖](https://www.inference.org.uk/mackay/itila/) |

## Probabilistic ML, Graphical Models, Inference & Generative Models

*Modeling the world as distributions, the dependency structure between variables, and how to do inference over them — the probabilistic backbone of ML and the foundation under generative models. (Bishop PRML, Murphy PML, and Gelman BDA3, above, are the broad references.)*

**Graphical models & probabilistic ML**

| Book | Why | |
|------|-----|---|
| Koller & Friedman — *Probabilistic Graphical Models* | the PGM bible — representation, inference, and learning over structured distributions | — |
| Barber — *Bayesian Reasoning and Machine Learning* | the most readable PGM / Bayesian-ML text | [📖](http://www.cs.ucl.ac.uk/staff/d.barber/brml/) |
| Rasmussen & Williams — *Gaussian Processes for Machine Learning* | the GP bible — Bayesian nonparametrics done right | [📖](http://www.gaussianprocess.org/gpml/) |

**Inference — variational & approximate**

| Book | Why | |

| Blei, Kucukelbir & McAuliffe — *Variational Inference: A Review for Statisticians* | the modern VI on-ramp (paper-length, essential) | [📖](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.00670) |

## Applied Math — Dynamical Systems, PDEs, Control & Statistical Physics

*The continuous-math backbone of modern ML: **ODEs/SDEs** (neural ODEs, diffusion, flow matching), **control & dynamical systems** (RL-as-control, world models, training dynamics), and **statistical physics** (energy-based models, the physics of learning). The 506 reading layer.*

**Differential equations & dynamical systems**

| Book | Why | |
|------|-----|---|

| Strogatz — *Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos* | the dynamical-systems classic — stability, bifurcations, attractors | — |
| Evans — *Partial Differential Equations* | the graduate PDE standard (for PINNs, neural operators, diffusion) | — |
