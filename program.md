# The Nth Experiment — 8-Month Self-Directed Diploma

**Duration:** May 2026 → January 2027
**Mission:** Go deep on what genuinely interests me. Curiosity-led, experiment-driven, no performance for anyone else.

## Rules of Engagement
- One active experiment per subject at a time
- Log when something is worth recording — insight, confusion, breakthrough, a question that won't leave
- Reflect before moving to the next experiment. The reflection seeds the next one
- Done > perfect. Abandon experiments that aren't working; note why
- This is for me. Not for the world to see
