# The Nth Experiment — 8-Month Self-Directed Diploma

**Duration:** May 2026 → January 2027
**Mission:** Go deep on what genuinely interests me. Curiosity-led, experiment-driven, no performance for anyone else.

## The Ethos
Learning here means *breaking things to understand them*. Every experiment starts with a claim — something a paper asserts, a book argues, or intuition suggests — and the job is to test it: build the minimal thing that proves or disproves it, observe what actually happens, and extract something reusable from the wreckage.

Reading without building is not enough. Understanding is only real when you can reconstruct it from scratch, break it deliberately, and explain why it breaks.

## Rules of Engagement
- Start with a claim, not a topic. "I want to understand X" is not an experiment. "I want to test whether X holds when Y changes" is.
- Build the minimal thing that tests the claim. Don't over-engineer before you understand.
- Break it intentionally — ablate, stress-test, vary the conditions. Failure teaches more than success.
- Every experiment produces something reusable: code, a mental model, a methodology, a cleaned-up notebook.
- Reflect before moving on. The reflection is where understanding actually forms.
- One active experiment per subject at a time. Focus compounds.
- Abandon experiments that aren't teaching you anything — but write down why.
- This is for me. The standard is genuine understanding, not performance.
