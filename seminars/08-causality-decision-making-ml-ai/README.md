# Causality, Decision Making, ML and AI

The intersection of causal reasoning, decision theory, and machine learning. How do we build systems that can reason about interventions, make robust decisions, and handle distribution shift?

## Focus Areas
- Causal reinforcement learning: learning to intervene, not just predict
- Off-policy evaluation and counterfactual reasoning in decision systems
- Invariant risk minimization and causal features
- Structural causal models as a foundation for decision-making
- Causal discovery in high-dimensional settings
- Connections between causality, robustness, and out-of-distribution generalization
- Decision theory: utility, loss, and rational agency
