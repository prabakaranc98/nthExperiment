# Machine Learning and Deep Learning Theory

The rigorous mathematical core — statistical learning theory, generalization bounds, expressiveness results, and the theoretical explanations for why modern deep learning works.

## Focus Areas
- PAC learning and VC dimension
- Rademacher complexity and generalization bounds
- Kernel methods: SVMs, RKHSs, and the kernel trick
- Neural tangent kernel (NTK) regime
- Mean field theory of neural networks
- Implicit regularization and the implicit bias of SGD
- Expressiveness: circuit complexity, depth separation, approximation theory
- Benign overfitting and interpolation thresholds
