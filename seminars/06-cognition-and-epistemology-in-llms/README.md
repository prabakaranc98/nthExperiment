# Cognition and Epistemology in LLMs

Do LLMs know what they know? Do they reason, believe, understand — or simulate these things? This seminar sits at the intersection of cognitive science, philosophy of mind, and empirical AI research.

## Focus Areas
- Epistemology of LLMs: belief, knowledge, and justified assertion
- Theory of mind in language models: do they model others' mental states?
- Compositionality and systematic generalization
- Analogy, abstraction, and the limits of in-context learning
- Hallucination: its structure, causes, and connection to uncertainty
- Calibration: does confidence track accuracy?
- Grounding: what does it mean to "understand" a word?
