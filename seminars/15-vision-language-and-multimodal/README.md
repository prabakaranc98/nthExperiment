# Vision Language Modeling and Multimodal AI

How do systems learn to see and speak — and to reason across modalities? This seminar covers the architectures, training paradigms, and open problems in vision-language and multimodal models.

## Focus Areas
- Vision encoders: ViT, CLIP, DINOv2, SigLIP
- VLM architectures: LLaVA, Flamingo, InstructBLIP, Idefics
- Joint embedding spaces: contrastive, generative, and hybrid
- Image-text generation: tokenization of visual content
- Video understanding: temporal modeling, video-language alignment
- Multimodal reasoning: charts, diagrams, spatial reasoning
- Grounding: connecting language to visual referents
