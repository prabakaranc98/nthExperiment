# Representation Learning

What makes a good representation? This seminar covers how neural networks learn to encode structure, the geometry of learned embeddings, and the connection between representation and generalization.

## Focus Areas
- Autoencoders, VAEs, and the information bottleneck
- Contrastive learning: SimCLR, MoCo, BYOL
- Disentangled representations: theory and practice
- Self-supervised learning and pretext tasks
- Representation geometry: isotropy, alignment, uniformity
- Emergent representations in large models
- The connection between representation and causal structure
