# Foundations of Deep Learning and Neural Networks

The mathematical and conceptual bedrock — universal approximation, backpropagation, optimization theory, and the theoretical understanding of why deep learning works (and when it doesn't).

## Focus Areas
- Universal approximation theorems: what they say and what they don't
- Backpropagation: the algorithm and its subtleties
- Optimization theory: convergence, saddle points, loss landscape
- Bias-variance tradeoff in the overparameterized regime
- PAC learning and statistical learning theory
- Information-theoretic perspectives on learning
- Depth separation: what deep networks can do that shallow ones cannot
