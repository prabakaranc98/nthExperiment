# Modern Deep Learning — Architectures, Mechanics and Dynamics

Going deep on how neural networks actually behave: optimization dynamics, loss landscapes, generalization theory, and the design principles behind modern architectures.

## Focus Areas
- Optimization: SGD, Adam, learning rate schedules, loss landscape geometry
- Generalization: double descent, neural scaling, implicit regularization
- Normalization: BatchNorm, LayerNorm, RMSNorm — why they work
- Residual connections and depth: what depth actually buys
- Initialization and training dynamics (NTK, mean field theory)
- Architecture design: MLPs, CNNs, RNNs, Transformers — comparative mechanics
- Neural ODEs and continuous-depth networks
