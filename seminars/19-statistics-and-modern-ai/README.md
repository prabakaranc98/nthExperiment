# Statistics and Modern AI

The statistical foundations beneath modern AI — probability theory, estimation, hypothesis testing, Bayesian reasoning, and how classical statistics relates to (and is being transformed by) large-scale learning.

## Focus Areas
- Probability theory: measure theory foundations, concentration inequalities
- Estimation theory: MLE, MAP, bias-variance, Cramér-Rao
- Bayesian inference: priors, posteriors, MCMC, variational inference
- Hypothesis testing and the limits of p-values
- High-dimensional statistics: the curse of dimensionality, sparsity, lasso
- Statistics of LLMs: in-context learning as Bayesian inference?
- Conformal prediction and distribution-free uncertainty quantification
- Multiple testing, false discovery rate
