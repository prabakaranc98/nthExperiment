# Generative AI and LLMs — Post-training

Pre-training gives a model knowledge. Post-training gives it behavior. This seminar covers the full post-training stack: RLHF, DPO, instruction tuning, alignment, preference optimization, and the emerging science of steering models after training.

## Focus Areas
- Instruction tuning and supervised fine-tuning (SFT)
- Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)
- Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) and variants
- Constitutional AI and self-critique
- Alignment: what it means and how it fails
- Steering vectors, activation editing, representation engineering
- Evals and benchmarking post-trained models
