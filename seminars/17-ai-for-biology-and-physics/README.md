# AI for Biology and Physics

Where deep learning meets the natural sciences. Structure prediction, molecular design, physics-informed neural networks, and the question of whether AI can actually accelerate scientific discovery.

## Focus Areas
- Protein structure prediction: AlphaFold, ESM, RoseTTAFold
- Molecular generation and drug discovery
- Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs): enforcing physical laws
- Neural operators: FNO, DeepONet — learning solution operators
- Symmetry and equivariance: E(3)-equivariant networks
- Scientific foundation models
- Evaluation: what does it mean to "solve" a scientific problem?
