# Reinforcement Learning for AI/DL and Applied Research

> I am deeply curious about reinforcement learning as the framework for sequential decision-making under uncertainty — and specifically how RL is reshaping modern AI: from post-training LLMs to scientific discovery to autonomous systems.

## Core Questions
- What problems is RL uniquely suited for that supervised learning cannot solve?
- How do policy gradient, value-based, and model-based methods compare in practice?
- How is RL being used to train LLMs to reason, and what are the limits?
- What are the failure modes of RL at scale, and how do you diagnose them?
