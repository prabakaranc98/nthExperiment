# History & Theory of Intelligence

> I am deeply curious about what intelligence actually is — across the natural and artificial. How consciousness works, how complexity gives rise to mind, what epistemology tells us about knowing, and where this whole trajectory is heading.

## Core Questions
- What is intelligence, really? (Not a definition — a genuine inquiry)
- How does complexity produce mind?
- What does it mean for something to "know"?
- How far have we actually come, and what are we building toward?
