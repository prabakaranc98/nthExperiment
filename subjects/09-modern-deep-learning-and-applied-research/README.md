# Modern Deep Learning and Applied Research

> I am deeply curious about what actually works in practice — how the best applied researchers think, how to go from a paper to a working implementation, and how to build judgment about when to trust a result.

## Core Questions
- How do you evaluate a deep learning result honestly?
- What separates applied research from engineering, and why does it matter?
- What are the recurring patterns of failure in ML experiments?
- How do you design an experiment that teaches you something real?
