# Psychology & Behavioral Economics

> I am deeply curious about how people actually think and decide — the gap between what humans say and what they do, the forces that distort judgment, and how understanding this sharpens my own thinking.

## Core Questions
- What biases and heuristics shape decisions without people noticing?
- What actually drives motivation and behavior change?
- How do social systems distort individual judgment?
- How do I apply this to my own reasoning and habits?
