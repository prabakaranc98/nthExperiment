# Causal AI

> I am deeply curious about causality as the gap between pattern-matching and real understanding — how intervention and counterfactual reasoning work, and how to build systems that can think about "what if" rather than just "what was."

## Core Questions
- What is the difference between correlation and causation, formally?
- How do you reason about interventions on systems you can't fully observe?
- Can causal reasoning be learned, or must it be specified?
- Where does causality meet decision-making and planning?
