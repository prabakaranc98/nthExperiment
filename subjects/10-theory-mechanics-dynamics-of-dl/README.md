# Theory, Mechanics and Dynamics of Deep Learning

> I am deeply curious about why deep learning actually works — the optimization geometry, the implicit biases, the dynamical systems view of training, and the gap between what theory predicts and what practitioners observe.

## Core Questions
- What is a neural network really doing when it trains?
- Why do overparameterized models generalize?
- What does the loss landscape look like, and why does SGD find good solutions?
- What is the relationship between architecture and the dynamics of learning?
