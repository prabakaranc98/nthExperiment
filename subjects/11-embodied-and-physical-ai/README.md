# Embodied and Physical AI

> I am deeply curious about intelligence that is grounded in a body and a physical world — how perception, action, and environment shape cognition, and whether disembodied AI can ever reach the same kind of understanding.

## Core Questions
- Does intelligence require a body, or is it substrate-independent?
- How do physics simulators and real-world interaction change what a system can learn?
- What is the right interface between deep learning and physical simulation?
- How does embodied cognition in biology inform the design of robots and physical agents?
