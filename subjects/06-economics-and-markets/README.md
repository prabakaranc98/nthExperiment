# Economics & Markets

> I am deeply curious about how economies actually work — the historical record of how technology, wealth, and power reshape civilizations — and what AI specifically does to that story going forward.

## Core Questions
- How do markets process information and coordinate action?
- How did previous technological transitions actually unfold?
- What changes when cognition scales like a commodity?
- How do I think about independence and building in this environment?

## Essential Starting Points

- [ ] **The Wealth of Nations — Adam Smith** *(selected)*
  - Book I, Ch. 1–3: Division of Labour
  - Book I, Ch. 5–7: Value, Price, and Wages
  - Book IV, Ch. 9: Conclusion (on systems of political economy)

- [ ] **The Road to Serfdom — Hayek**
  - Ch. 3: Individualism and Collectivism
  - Ch. 5: Planning and Democracy
  - Ch. 10: Why the Worst Get on Top

- [ ] **The Innovator's Dilemma — Christensen**
  - Part I, Ch. 1–4: Why great firms fail (disruptive vs sustaining)
  - Part II, Ch. 8–9: Managing disruptive technological change

- [ ] **Power and Progress — Acemoglu & Johnson**
  - Ch. 1: The Choice (technology doesn't determine outcomes)
  - Ch. 4: Artificial Slaves
  - Ch. 9–10: Redirecting AI

- [ ] **Poor Charlie's Almanack — Charlie Munger**
  - Talk 7: The Psychology of Human Misjudgment (full)
  - Talk 11: Practical Thought About Practical Thought
